# AI Use Log

AI was used in this project mainly as a guidance and research-support tool rather than as a replacement for our own work or analysis.

In the early stage of the project, we used AI to ask whether data for the variables we were considering was available and which indicators could potentially be appropriate for our research. This gave us an initial direction, but we then checked the availability and suitability of the variables ourselves using the World Development Indicators and other relevant sources.

We also used AI to discuss the handling of missing values. I already had previous familiarity with linear interpolation, but I used AI to confirm whether it could be an appropriate method for filling missing observations between available years in our dataset. We examined the missing-value patterns ourselves and made the final decisions about which variables and observations could reasonably be retained, filled, or removed.

AI was also used to help make some of our own written responses more readable. We provided our own ideas, reasoning, and paragraphs and used AI mainly to improve their clarity and readability. We reviewed the resulting wording to make sure that it still represented our actual work and reasoning.

We did not use AI to generate unverified statistics or results. Information suggested by AI was checked against the relevant data sources before being used. Any cleaning decisions, interpretations, and final analysis were made by us based on our examination of the data.
